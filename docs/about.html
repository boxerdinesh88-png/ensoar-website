Who We Are

A DSA that works for the customer

Ensoar Pvt. Ltd. is a registered company working as a Direct Selling Agent (DSA) for vehicle finance. We are the bridge between borrowers and lenders — understanding your profile, matching it with the right bank or NBFC, and managing the file end-to-end until the loan is disbursed.

Buying a vehicle is a big decision. Different lenders have different rates, eligibility rules and processing times. Our job is to remove the confusion, save your time and get you the best deal your profile qualifies for.

  • Salaried, self-employed, professionals & farmers
  • New, used & commercial vehicles, two-wheelers & EVs
  • Support for first-time borrowers and low CIBIL cases*
