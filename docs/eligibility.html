Eligibility – Salaried

  • Age 21 – 60 years (at loan maturity)
  • Minimum net salary ₹15,000/month*
  • At least 1 year total work experience
  • 6 months in current job
  • CIBIL score 650+ preferred

Documents Required

  • PAN card & Aadhaar card
  • Passport size photographs
  • Last 3 months salary slips
  • Last 6 months bank statement
  • Form 16 / ITR (for higher amounts)
  • Address proof (if different from Aadhaar)

Eligibility – Self-Employed

  • Age 21 – 65 years (at loan maturity)
  • Business stable for 2+ years
  • Annual income ₹2 lakh+*
  • Own house or office preferred
  • CIBIL score 650+ preferred

Documents Required

  • PAN card & Aadhaar card
  • Passport size photographs
  • Last 2 years ITR with computation
  • Last 12 months bank statement
  • Business proof (GST / Udyam / Shop Act)
  • Office & residence address proof

Eligibility – Commercial Vehicle

  • Age 21 – 65 years
  • Transport experience or first-time buyer scheme*
  • Guarantor may be required
  • Valid driving licence (for owner-drivers)
  • Route / contract details helpful

Documents Required

  • KYC: PAN, Aadhaar, photographs
  • Last 6–12 months bank statement
  • Existing vehicle RCs (fleet owners)
  • ITR / income proof (if available)
  • Quotation / proforma invoice
  • Residence ownership proof

*Criteria vary by lender and product. Our advisor will confirm the exact list for your case.
