What is a DSA and how does Ensoar help me?

A Direct Selling Agent (DSA) is authorised to source loan applications for banks and NBFCs. Ensoar understands your requirement, compares lenders, prepares your file and coordinates until the loan is disbursed — saving you time and helping you get a better deal.

Do I have to pay Ensoar any fee?

No. Our consultation is free and we never ask for any upfront fee to sanction a loan. Only the lender's official processing fee, stamp duty and other charges (as per their policy) apply, and these are explained to you in advance.

How much loan can I get for a new car?

Depending on your income and credit profile, lenders may fund up to 100% of the on-road price for selected models. Typically, 80–90% funding is common for most profiles.

How long does approval take?

With complete documents, many cases get sanctioned within 24–48 hours. Commercial vehicle and self-employed cases may take a little longer due to verification.

Can I get a loan with a low CIBIL score?

It may be possible. Some lenders offer programs for low-score or new-to-credit customers, sometimes with a co-applicant or higher down payment. Share your details and we will check the options.

Can I get a loan for a car bought from an individual seller?

Yes. Used car loans are available for both dealer and private purchases. We guide you through valuation and RC transfer.

Can I prepay or foreclose my vehicle loan?

Yes, most lenders allow part-prepayment and foreclosure after a minimum number of EMIs. Charges vary by lender and are shared before you sign.

Is my personal information safe with Ensoar?

Yes. Your documents are used only for processing your loan application with the chosen lender and are never sold or shared with unrelated parties.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Indirapuram, Ghaziabad and serve customers across Delhi NCR — Ghaziabad, Noida, Greater Noida, Delhi, and nearby areas of Uttar Pradesh. Contact us to check service in your city.
